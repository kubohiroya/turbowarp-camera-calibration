TurboWarp-Camera-Calibration

Calibrate a camera shared through TurboWarp-Camera-Source and publish its intrinsic calibration profile.

Status: the calibration path ships behind a startup feature flag that defaults to OFF. With the flag OFF the extension publishes only the state reporter, which answers idle.

Requirements

  • TurboWarp Desktop, Web, or Packager.
  • @kubohiroya/turbowarp-camera-source loaded first. This extension never opens a camera itself.
  • A printed chessboard calibration target, flat and unglossy. The block arguments count inner corners, not printed squares: a 10×7 square board is 9 by 6.
  • The extension must run without the sandbox.

Calibrating a camera

start shared camera [default]
start camera [default] calibration [calibration-1] board [9] by [6] square [0.025] m max error [1.5] px
repeat until <camera [default] calibration sample count = 12>
  add calibration sample for camera [default]
solve calibration for camera [default]
publish calibration profile for camera [default]

Each shared camera has its own session, profile, and diagnostics. Cancelling one camera never stops another consumer of the same shared camera.

Getting a usable result

Do thisWhy
Capture at least 8 accepted samples, at most 40Fewer views leave the distortion terms under-determined
Vary the board angle by roughly 20°–45° across samplesViews that are all fronto-parallel cannot separate focal length from distance
Vary the distance and fill different parts of the frame, including the cornersDistortion is strongest away from the image center
Keep the whole board inside the frame and in focusA partially visible or blurred board is refused
Do not change resolution, camera device, mirroring, focus, or zoom during a sessionThe session is fixed to the resolution it started with

Verify a finished calibration against images that were not part of the solve. A low reprojection error on the calibration samples alone does not prove the result generalizes.

The calibration profile

The profile is intrinsics only: an intrinsic matrix, a distortion model and its coefficients, the calibrated image size, and when it was measured. It never carries a world pose, and a pose that was not measured is never filled in with identity. Preview mirroring is a display concern; profile coordinates are always the unmirrored capture coordinates.

Profiles written by @kubohiroya/turbowarp-realtime-motion-capture are accepted on import; their world pose is dropped rather than reinterpreted.

How the packages divide the work

PackageOwns
@kubohiroya/turbowarp-camera-source Camera acquisition, lease sharing, preview, and the calibration profile contract
@kubohiroya/turbowarp-camera-calibration The calibration procedure: chessboard sampling, solving, and profile publication

The OpenCV build needed to solve a calibration is roughly 10 MB, and a TurboWarp extension ships as a single standalone bundle. Keeping the solver in its own extension lets Camera Source stay small enough for every camera consumer to load.

Safety

Calibration reads camera frames only to detect the chessboard target. Nothing is uploaded. A profile that carries a pairing credential is rejected rather than stored. Load unsandboxed extension code only from a source you trust.
